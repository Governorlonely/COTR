MISSIONS.

LEADERSHIP TRAINING & CHURCH PLANTING

Alongside his pastoral work in North Houston, Pastor Sergei Gorokhov serves churches in Kazan, Yoshkar-Ola, and Cheboksary in Russia’s Volga region through pastoral mentoring, leadership training, and church planting.

Regular Sunday services began at the new church in Cheboksary in 2026. Pastor Sergei continues to support its development while helping strengthen the established churches in Kazan and Yoshkar-Ola.

His plans for 2027 include two ministry trips for church visits and pastoral and leadership conferences, along with the launch of a yearlong online training program developed with local ministry teams and focused on practical church ministry.

Giving to this work supports Pastor Sergei’s ministry travel and operating expenses. This support is separate from support for our North Houston ministry.
