Our story

About Us

We are a church in North Houston where you will meet people from Ukraine, Russia, Belarus, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Armenia, and other nations.

We value cultural diversity and are building an open community where you can find friends, grow in faith, and feel right at home.

Our conviction

True life starts with a relationship with God.

Therefore we seek to build a church that is:

01

A Place of Inspiration

02

A Place for Everyone

Where every person matters and is welcomed — regardless of background, culture, or past.

03

A Place of Growth

Where we grow spiritually, strengthen families, and bring out the best God has placed within us.

Statement of faith

What We Believe

Click on any topic below to read more.

01Historic Creeds

We affirm and share the historic Christian creeds of the Church:

  • The Apostles’ Creed;
  • The Nicene Creed.
02Holy Scripture

We believe that the 66 books of Holy Scripture are the inspired, infallible, and inerrant Word of God given to humanity.

Apocryphal and other historic Christian writings may hold spiritual and historical value, yet they do not carry the authority of Holy Scripture and must be evaluated in the light of the Bible.

03The Trinity

We believe in One God eternally existing in three Persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

We reject modalism — the view that Father, Son, and Holy Spirit are merely different modes of a single Person. The Trinity is three distinct Persons dwelling in perfect unity of will, nature, and purpose while fulfilling distinct roles in God’s plan.

In the work of salvation:

  • The Father is the Source and Author of salvation;
  • The Son is the Savior and Redeemer;
  • The Holy Spirit is the One who makes salvation real and active in a person’s life.

We believe that Jesus Christ is truly God and truly Man — fully God and fully human.

We believe that the Holy Spirit is not an impersonal force, but a real Divine Person.

04Salvation

We believe that salvation is given solely by God’s grace through faith in Jesus Christ as Lord and Savior.

Good works are the fruit of salvation, not its cause.

Salvation is received through repentance and faith. Genuine repentance is evidenced by devotion to Christ, obedience to His Word, and a desire to live according to His will.

05Water Baptism

Water baptism is an ordinance of the Church.

We do not pit baptism “in the name of Jesus” against baptism “in the name of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit.” The person being baptized confesses faith in Jesus Christ as Lord and Savior, affirms the biblical teaching of the Trinity, and pledges a good conscience toward God.

Baptism does not signify sinless perfection, but expresses a conscious decision to live for the Lord and follow Him.

We believe water baptism should not be unnecessarily delayed, while ensuring that the believer understands the foundations of Christian faith and consciously dedicates their life to Christ.

06Communion

Communion (the Lord’s Supper) is an ordinance of the Church intended for believers.

We seek to help believers understand the meaning of the bread and the cup as symbols of the redemptive sacrifice of Jesus Christ — receiving God’s forgiveness, standing firm in Christ’s righteousness, and growing in the hope of eternal life.

07The Holy Spirit, Spiritual Gifts, Miracles & Healing

We believe that the ministry and work of the Holy Spirit continue today.

We do not hold that miracles, spiritual gifts, and God’s supernatural work ceased with the apostolic age. God continues to work among His people — healing, delivering, guiding, and equipping believers with spiritual gifts.

We encourage Christians to be filled with the Holy Spirit and eagerly desire spiritual gifts to serve God and others.

At the same time, miracles are never an end in themselves; their purpose is to point people to Christ and meet human need, never for spectacle or self-promotion.

We believe healing may occur instantaneously, gradually, or find its ultimate fulfillment in the resurrection and redemption of the body.

We also recognize the reality of spiritual warfare and deliverance ministry, always paired with pastoral care, discipleship, and ongoing spiritual growth.

08The Second Coming & Eternity

We believe that Jesus Christ will return to judge the living and the dead.

Every person will stand before God’s judgment — some unto eternal salvation and life with God, others unto eternal judgment.

On secondary eschatological details, we allow room for differing interpretations while holding firmly to historic Christian orthodoxy.

Our primary emphasis is living in continual readiness for Christ’s return at any moment.

We believe in a new heaven and a new earth, the renewal of all creation, and resurrection life with God forever where there will be no more sickness, death, mourning, or pain.
