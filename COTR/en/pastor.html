Our story

Sergei & Nina Gorokhov

We believe that church begins with relationships. That is why we want not just to tell you about ourselves, but to get to know you.

For over 20 years our lives have been dedicated to serving people and developing churches. Over that time we have helped people grow in faith, build strong families, and find their place in life and ministry.

We have been married for 25 years and are raising three children. Family, relationships, and parenting are not merely topics we speak about — they are a vital part of our own everyday life.

Today we are building a church community in North Houston. Moving to another country ourselves has helped us understand even more deeply how important it is to be among people who know you, support you, and help you feel at home.

That is the kind of church we are committed to building here — open to people from many nations, marked by genuine relationships, living faith, and a place for everyone.
