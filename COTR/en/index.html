About Us

About Us

Different backgrounds. One family.

We are a church in North Houston where you will meet people from Ukraine, Russia, Belarus, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Armenia, and other nations.

We value cultural diversity and are building an open community where you can find friends, grow in faith, and feel right at home.

Therefore we seek to build a church that is:

01

A Place of Inspiration

Where faith helps us see further, dream bolder, and live with purpose.

02

A Place for Everyone

Where every person matters and is welcomed — regardless of background, culture, or past.

03

A Place of Growth

Where we grow spiritually, strengthen families, and bring out the best God has placed within us.
