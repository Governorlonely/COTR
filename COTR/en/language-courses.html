Language Classes

Learning to communicate, making friends, and becoming part of a community.

More than words

Language Helps Build Relationships

We believe language helps us not only communicate, but also build lasting relationships.

Our language classes help newcomers feel more confident in a new country, meet people, make friends, and communicate freely in everyday life.

01

For adults

English Club

Conversational English

If you want to speak English with greater confidence, overcome the language barrier, and practice in real-life conversation — our classes are for you.

We offer beginner and intermediate groups in a warm, welcoming small-group setting.

You will be able to

  • Improve your conversational English
  • Expand your everyday vocabulary
  • Practice without fear of making mistakes
  • Meet new people and build friendships
02

For kids

Russian & Ukrainian for Kids

Helping children preserve their heritage language

We want children to speak Russian or Ukrainian fluently, communicate easily with extended family, and stay connected to their cultural heritage.

In class, kids learn to read, write, develop speech, and interact with peers in an encouraging environment.
