Frequently asked questions about Sunday gatherings

01

How long is the service?

About 1 hour and 15 minutes.

02

What language is the service in?

The primary language is Russian, with translation into English, Ukrainian, and other languages.

03

Who is this gathering for?

Everyone is welcome — whether you are already part of a church, exploring faith, or simply looking to meet people.

04

What about children?

Childcare and kids’ activities are provided during the gathering so parents can comfortably participate in the service.

05

What should I wear?

Come dressed however you feel comfortable. There is no dress code.

06

Do I need to register in advance?

Advance registration is not required — you are always welcome to just show up.

However, if you plan your visit ahead of time, our team will know you are coming, meet you at the entrance, help you get oriented, and prepare a small welcome gift for you.

07

Does it cost anything?

No. Attendance is completely free.

08

What if it’s my first time at church?

Nothing special is expected of you. Simply come — we will be glad to welcome you.

09

Where do I park and where should I go?

There is plenty of free parking on campus at the address listed above. Pick any convenient spot and follow the signs to Equipping Center 1 (EC1).
